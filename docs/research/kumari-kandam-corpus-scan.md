## Interpretation

The Sangam poems know **Cape Kumari** as a southern limit and **Kumari** as a
young woman or a place/river. They do not describe a submerged Tamil continent.
The Kumari Kandam narrative belongs to later Tamil tradition and modern
historiography; Lemuria belongs to nineteenth-century colonial science. Both
must be dated and labelled separately from the Sangam corpus.
