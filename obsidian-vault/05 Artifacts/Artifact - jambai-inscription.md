# ஜம்பைக் கல்வெட்டு · Jambai Inscription

- Class: **epigraphy / Tamil Brahmi hero-stone inscription**
- Period: c. 1st–2nd century CE
- Place: Jambai, Villupuram district
- Script: Tamil Brahmi
- Rights: open-metadata

A celebrated early Tamil-Brahmi inscription associated with a Chola chief and the hero-stone tradition; important for the transition from cave labels to public memorial inscriptions.
