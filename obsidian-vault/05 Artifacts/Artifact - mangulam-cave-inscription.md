# மாங்குளம் குகைக் கல்வெட்டு · Mangulam Cave Inscription

- Class: **epigraphy / Tamil Brahmi cave inscription**
- Period: c. 3rd–2nd century BCE
- Place: Mangulam, Madurai district
- Script: Tamil Brahmi
- Rights: open-metadata

One of the earliest dated Tamil-Brahmi cave inscriptions, associated with Jain ascetics and the Pandya chief Nedunjeliyan. A foundational object for the history of Tamil writing.
