# யாழ் இசைக்கருவி · Yazh (Reconstruction)

- Class: **music / stringed instrument**
- Period: Sangam–medieval
- Place: Tamil country
- Script: —
- Rights: open-metadata

The yazh is the harp of Sangam poetry, central to pann music and the imagery of Tamil love and war. No complete ancient instrument survives; reconstructions are interpretive.
