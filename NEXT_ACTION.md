# Founder Refresher & Next Actions — Open Sangam

> **Milestone Expectations:**
> - 🎯 **October 2026:** Pilot Release — Complete 18 Sangam Anthologies & Avai Poet Agents Interactive Demo
> - 🚀 **December 2026:** Public Launch — Full "Duolingo for Ancient Literature" with 3D Tiṇai Landscape, Multi-layered Translation & Knowledge Graph

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Vision

Open Sangam bridges Classical Tamil literature with modern readers through interactive learning, word-by-word morphological breakdowns (*Urichol*), layered modern prose (*Urai*), and historical context.

---

## 2. October 2026 Pilot Scope

- [ ] **Corpus & Translation Integrity:**
  - Complete verification across all 18 primary anthologies (Ettuthokai & Pathupattu).
  - Synchronize modern Tamil and English layered verse translations.
- [ ] **Avai Poet Agent Interface:**
  - Live conversational UI connecting poets (Kapilar, Paranar, Avvaiyar) with real-time verse explanation APIs.
- [ ] **Morphology & Lexicon (Urichol):**
  - Instant word-click definitions and etymological popovers across key poem collections.

---

## 3. December 2026 Launch Scope

- [ ] **Sangam World 3D / Tiṇai Navigator:**

  - Interactive web graph connecting poets, patron-kings, flora/fauna (*Karu-porul*), and cultural references.
- [ ] **Mobile & Offline Experience:**
  - PWA packaging with offline reader caching and fast lexical lookup.
